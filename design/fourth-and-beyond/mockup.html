The gap

The focus falls entirely on the baby.

Becoming a new parent is an incredible journey. Yet almost all the preparation, the appointments and the advice point one way — leaving mothers and birth parents unprepared and overlooked in their own recovery.

The cost of that oversight is immense: mental health struggles, and a difficult physical return to health.

You deserve to thrive, not just survive.

The kits

Immediate, clinically‑reviewed care — already in the house.

Clinically reviewed

Every item and every piece of guidance is checked by clinicians, so you are not sorting truth from noise at three in the morning.

It arrives before you need it
