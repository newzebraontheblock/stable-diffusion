The focus falls entirely on the baby.

Nobody tells you what’s normal

Bleeding, cramping, night sweats. Most people find out by searching at three in the morning.

Support stops at six weeks

The fourth trimester runs twelve. And some care only arrives if you know to ask for it.

Everyone buys for the baby

The babygrows, the blankets, the tiny shoes. Almost nothing for the person recovering.

The research

Mothers know best

We asked over 130 mothers what they actually needed after giving birth. They told us what they wished someone had given them.
